What it does that others do not

Never heavier

JPEG is bad at flat colour and text. When the result would weigh more than the original, nothing is written and it says so — the lighter file was already on your disk.

Transparency survives

A PNG with transparent pixels is never flattened onto white. It is shrunk as a PNG instead, losslessly: pixels and alpha come back byte for byte identical.

Nothing tags along

No Exif, no GPS, no camera model, no colour profile, no thumbnail. The rotation a phone asks for is applied to the pixels, then thrown away with the rest.

Colour by content

Chroma subsampling is chosen by measuring the picture's hard colour transitions — the thing 4:2:0 destroys. Photographs score zero, a screenshot 3.5%.

Under the hood

The smallest JPEG jpegli can write

Every switch was measured. --xyb is 24% smaller and rejected: a viewer that ignores its colour profile renders an average error of 34.78 against 0.88. The progressive level, which changes no pixel at all, sat at a default that was never the smallest of the three — worth 1 to 7%.

Corners identical to the byte

GDI+ rasterises the right and bottom of a shape differently from the left and top. Every rounded shape is drawn once and its top-left quarter mirrored over the other three, so all four corners measure 0 difference.

Drawn at your screen's real resolution

The window asks Windows for per-monitor DPI awareness rather than being stretched up from 96 DPI, and one factor sets the fonts, every control and every radius — capped by what the screen can actually hold.

When something goes wrong, there is something to read

A log holds the machine, the settings in force, every failure with its reason and a summary of every batch. Nothing waits for ever: the encoder gets two minutes, the WebP tools thirty seconds.

Linux and macOS

Experimental, and never run by its author. The same right-click entry for GNOME Files, KDE Dolphin, XFCE Thunar and the macOS Finder lives in platform/. It was written on Windows: the shell parses and the property lists are valid XML, and that is the whole of what has been checked.
